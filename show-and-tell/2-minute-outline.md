# the halluCinators: 2-minute show and tell

Presenter: Carl McCullagh. About 250 spoken words. Show the prototype from 0:20.

## 0:00 to 1:00 · What we built, and for whom

- **Challenge 2: help when it's needed.** Most drug and alcohol support closes at 5pm, but people are most alone at night. About 60% of people who died a drug-related death in Scotland lived alone. Meet Gary: on his own on a Saturday night, struggling, and not wanting to go straight to 999.
- **Still here helps Gary reach his own people first.** He can open a tiny page, no sign-up, and tap "I need someone". Or he gets an 11pm check-in **by text**: reply 1, 2 or 3, no data needed. With his say-so, it texts Lorraine, then Mick. If nobody picks up, Jo, a volunteer supporter, offers to call. Then services open now. It never calls 999 for him, and there's a quick exit on every screen. *(Demo: **I need help right now** → yes to a call → Sam's card.)*
- **Low data, low battery, poor signal or an old phone? He can call or text Still here instead and get all the same help.** No app, no data, no smartphone needed. *(Demo: `phone.html` → **Text** → HELP.)*

## 1:00 to 1:30 · One thing that didn't work

- **Flows that looked finished didn't hold up until we walked through them as Gary.** The check-in text dropped him onto a web page, which needs the data he hasn't got. Inviting Lorraine acted as if she'd already said yes. And our name, "Alex", clashed with real people's names, so we went back to "Still here".
- Only walking through it together, as the person we're helping, caught these.

## 1:30 to 2:00 · One surprise from the tooling

- **Everyone built, outside their job.** Patricia, our interaction designer, built the supporter journey and restyled the app. Anastasia, in QA, fixed bugs herself, and Claude ran 60+ test scenarios for her. I'm a product manager, and I built the text check-in.
- **One prompt kept six of us out of each other's way.** Elena asked Claude: "we need to split the tasks between all of us". It gave each journey its own file, and we had hardly any clashes.
