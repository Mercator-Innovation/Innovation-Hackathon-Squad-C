# Sign-up for the person: steps and words (draft)

Joseph Thrush. For `src/signup.js`, once Spencer's hooks are in. Made-up names only.

## On every sign-up screen

- **Quick exit** stays in the top corner, as now.
- **Talk to someone now, no name needed**: on every step, a way straight to anonymous contact with a volunteer supporter who's on now. No sign-up, no name, no number. Nothing typed so far is kept. (It uses Spencer's trap door, which asks for a name: this needs it to work with no name at all. Agree with Spencer and Elena.)
  - **A made-up name in one tap.** If a name is asked for, offer "Give me a made-up name". It picks a word pair, such as "Blue Fox" or "Quiet River": clearly made up, easy to remember, friendly. They can tap again for another, or type their own.
  - The volunteer sees: "You're talking to Blue Fox."
- **Skip** on every step. Nothing is required.

## 1. Before you start

**Set up Still here**
Best done when you're feeling OK. It takes about 2 minutes, and you can skip any step.
We never tell anyone anything without asking you first.
If you need someone now, tap "Talk to someone now".

- Start
- Talk to someone now, no name needed
- Not now

### Setting it up together

Someone they trust, like Lorraine, can sit with them and help. But **the person is always the one who agrees**, on their own phone, so no one can sign someone up behind their back. (Overlaps with Patricia's supporter journey: check with her.)

- Option on this screen: "Someone's helping me set this up"
- Then: **Good to have help.** You'll still be the one who says yes at each step.

## 2. Your name

**What should we call you?**
Any name is fine. It doesn't have to be your real one.

- Label: Your name
- Next / Skip

## 3. People you trust

**Who would you like to reach when you need someone?**
Pick people you trust. We'll ask them first, and they can say no.

- Label: Their name
- Label: Who they are to you (for example, sister or friend)
- Label: Their phone number
- Add this person / Skip for now

After adding: "You've added Lorraine. Add someone else?" (up to 3)

## 4. Your message

**What should the message say?**
We'll always ask you before we send it. You can change it any time.

- Ready to use: "Hi, it's {name}. I'm not doing great tonight. Can you call me?"
- Use this / Change it / Skip

## 5. Asking your people

**We've asked Lorraine.**
She'll get a text asking if she's happy to be someone you can reach. We won't add her unless she says yes.

What Lorraine sees:
> {name} would like you to be someone they can reach at night, using Still here. You can say no, and you can stop at any time. Reply YES or NO.

Demo buttons:
- Demo: Lorraine says yes → **Lorraine said yes.** She's one of your people now.
- Demo: Lorraine says no → **Lorraine can't right now. That's OK.** You can ask someone else, or a volunteer supporter can be there instead.

## 6. Done

**You're set up, {name}.**
When you open Still here, we'll ask how you are. You decide who's told, every time.

- Go to Still here

## If they skip everything

**That's fine.** You can still use Still here. We'll show a banner later: "Want to add people you trust?"
